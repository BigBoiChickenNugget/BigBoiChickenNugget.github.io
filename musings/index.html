Musings

✦

Notes, ideas, and writeups that live somewhere between coursework and finished projects.

I want this section to capture the practical side of learning embedded systems: the weird bugs, the design tradeoffs, the parts that didn't work the first time, and the details that finally made something click.

What will show up here

  • Firmware bring-up lessons from STM32 and ESP32 projects.
  • PCB design notes from telemetry, sensing, and communication hardware.
  • Control tuning and instrumentation thoughts from robotics work.
  • Reflections on student design teams, engineering process, and system tradeoffs.
  • Media and aesthetics that influence how I present technical work.

Why bother writing it down

Not every useful thing belongs on a polished project page. Sometimes the most valuable takeaway is a failed test, an ugly workaround, or a debugging habit that saved hours later.

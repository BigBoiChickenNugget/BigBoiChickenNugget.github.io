My Top 5 Favourite Games

✦

These are not necessarily the “best” games ever made. They’re just the ones that stayed with me the hardest and probably say the most about my taste.

1. Persona 5

Style carries this game a long way, but it is not just style. Persona 5 feels sharp in every direction: UI, music, pacing, mood, and identity. It is one of those rare games where the presentation is so confident that it becomes part of why the whole thing works.

2. Chrono Trigger

Chrono Trigger is one of those games that feels almost impossibly clean. No wasted motion, no bloated structure, no part of it overstaying its welcome. It is old in the best way: focused, elegant, and still easy to love.

3. Hollow Knight

Hollow Knight is probably the game on this list that feels the most atmospheric to me. The world is huge, lonely, and weirdly beautiful. It nails the feeling of getting lost in a place that does not care whether you understand it yet.

4. Celeste

Celeste is one of my favorite examples of difficulty being used with purpose. It is hard, but the kind of hard that teaches you how to keep moving. The movement feels amazing, and the emotional core is strong enough that the challenge never feels empty.

5. Danganronpa: Trigger Happy Havoc

Danganronpa is here because it is completely its own thing. The tone, the structure, the absurdity, the tension, the class trial energy — it all feels weird in a way I really respect. It is messy sometimes, but unforgettable.

What they have in common

Looking at the list, I think the pattern is pretty obvious: I love games with strong identity. If a game has memorable style, great music, a clear mood, and the confidence to feel like itself, I am probably already halfway in.

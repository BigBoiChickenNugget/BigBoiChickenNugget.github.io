Hi, I'm Talha.

✦

I'm a mechatronics engineering student at McMaster University focused on embedded systems, firmware, and the kind of hardware work that only gets fun once the debugging starts.

I like building systems that cross the software-hardware boundary: microcontrollers, control loops, communication buses, telemetry, and lightweight intelligence running close to the metal.

This site is where I collect the projects I'm proud of, the ideas I'm still thinking through, and some of the music, games, and aesthetics that keep me inspired while I build.

Right now I'm especially interested in embedded firmware, avionics, and reliable real-time systems.

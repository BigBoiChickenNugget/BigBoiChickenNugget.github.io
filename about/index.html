About

✦

I'm Talha Ahmad, a Toronto-based mechatronics engineering student at McMaster University. Most of what I build lives at the intersection of firmware, electronics, and systems thinking.

Embedded work is what excites me most. I like bringing up microcontrollers, interfacing with sensors, designing communication paths, and turning a rough prototype into something stable enough to trust.

Between internship work, design team projects, and personal builds, I've grown into the kind of engineer who enjoys both the low-level details and the bigger system around them.

What I care about

  • Embedded firmware on STM32 and ESP32-class hardware.
  • Communication and telemetry systems built around UART, I2C, RS-485, CAN, and MQTT.
  • Controls, sensing, and avionics-adjacent engineering.
  • Projects that feel technically solid and personally meaningful.

Technical toolkit

  • Languages: C, C++, Python, MATLAB, Java, and Rust.
  • Embedded and hardware: ESP32, STM32, PCB design in KiCad and Altium, FreeRTOS, and MbedOS.
  • Tools: Git, Docker, Linux, VS Code, soldering, and oscilloscope debugging.
  • CAD and mechanical support: Fusion 360, Inventor, and Onshape.

Outside engineering

The rest of this site's personality comes from the same things that shape how I like to build: JRPG soundtracks, anime, terminal aesthetics, and projects that have some character instead of feeling sterile.
